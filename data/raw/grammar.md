## Tense
Past motion often uses the verb form `ven`. Future/later time uses the adverb `pachi` rather than a dedicated tense suffix. The memo does not specify progressive aspect.

## Negation
Negation is a post-verbal or clause-final particle `na`.
Do not prefix English-style "not".

## Politeness and respect
When addressing an older sibling or higher-status kin, a respectful suffix `-ka` attaches to the kinship term (`bira-ka`).
Peer talk may omit `-ka` (see example E13).
The memo does not fully specify the form used immediately after a family reconciliation.
